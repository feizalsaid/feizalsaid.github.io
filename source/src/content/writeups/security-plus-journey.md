I passed Security+ in November 2025. Here's exactly how I prepared — no fluff, just what worked.

## Why Security+?

I wanted a foundational cert that actually matters. Security+ gave me the vocabulary to talk about risk, controls, and incidents without sounding like I just watched a YouTube video. It's not a deep technical cert, but it's a solid baseline.

## The Timeline: September - November 2025

| Month | What I Did |
|-------|-------------|
| Week 1-2 | Watched Professor Messer videos (1.5x speed) |
| Week 3-4 | Took notes on weak areas (crypto, PKI, ports) |
| Week 5-6 | ExamCompass practice tests — lots of them |
| Week 7 | Reviewed wrong answers, focused on acronyms |
| Week 8 (final week) | Practice exams, PBQ simulations, booked the test |

## Resources I Actually Used

### Primary Resources

**Professor Messer's SY0-701 Video Course** (Free)
- Watched all 100+ videos over two weeks
- 1.5x speed for review, normal speed for new concepts
- The course notes PDF was worth buying ($20)

**ExamCompass** (Free)
- 25+ practice tests covering specific domains
- Brutal but effective — harder than the real exam
- Questions repeat, so understand *why* the answer is correct

**CompTIA Security+ SY0-701 Exam Objectives** (Free)
- Printed the PDF. Marked topics as red/yellow/green.
- If you can explain every bullet point, you're ready.

### Secondary Resources

**Jason Dion Practice Exams** (Udemy, on sale ~$15)
- Good for PBQ (performance-based question) exposure
- Exams are slightly easier than ExamCompass

**Anki Flashcards** (Free)
- Made my own deck for ports (22, 443, 3389, etc.)
- Acronyms (RTO, MTTF, SLE, ALE, etc.)

**CyberKraft PBQ Simulations** (YouTube, Free)
- Best free PBQ walkthroughs
- Shows you exactly how the exam interface works

## My Study Schedule (Weekly Breakdown)

```markdown
Week 1-2: Professor Messer videos (1-2 hours daily)
- Domain 1.0: General Security Concepts
- Domain 2.0: Threats, Vulnerabilities, Mitigations
- Domain 3.0: Security Architecture

Week 3-4: Domain 4.0 & 5.0 + ExamCompass
- Domain 4.0: Security Operations
- Domain 5.0: Security Program Management
- ExamCompass tests for each domain

Week 5-6: Practice test marathon
- 50 questions daily (timed)
- Review every wrong answer
- Re-study weak domains

Week 7: Acronym memorization + PBQs
- 200+ acronyms down to 50 I kept missing
- CyberKraft PBQ videos

Week 8: Final review
- CompTIA practice exam (bought the official one)
- Reviewed my wrong answer log
- Booked the exam
